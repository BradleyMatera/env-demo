Frequently asked

Before you make the trip.

Does PCS LLC have a public showroom?

A public showroom has not been confirmed. Inspection and pickup details are arranged directly, so a private residence is not published.

Can Tyler help identify the right truck?

The site can invite buyers to describe the job, equipment, budget and timeline, but the exact services Tyler offers still need his approval.

Are warranties included?

No warranty should be advertised until Tyler provides and approves written terms for a specific sale.
