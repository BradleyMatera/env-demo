Listing standard

Enough detail to make a serious decision.

This design treats every unit as commercial equipment, not a generic vehicle card.

Identity

Year, make, model, chassis, VIN disclosure policy and title status.

Condition

Mileage or hours, known faults, corrosion, tires and recent work, only when Tyler confirms them.

Installed equipment

Manufacturer, model, reach or capacity, controls, accessories and known operating status.

Technical questions

What a commercial buyer needs.

What information should a listing include?

Tyler-approved chassis, mileage, equipment, title, known condition, price and inspection arrangements.

Does PCS LLC publish a home address?

No private home address is included. Inspection or pickup details are shared directly after contact.

Is every truck inspected or certified?

No inspection or certification claim should appear unless Tyler provides the exact process and approves the wording.

Accuracy rule: this concept is production-ready as a system, but the business facts and unit data remain placeholders until Tyler personally approves them.
