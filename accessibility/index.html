Accessibility

This website is designed for keyboard access, visible focus indicators, responsive text, readable contrast, meaningful headings, reduced-motion preferences and touch controls at least 44 pixels tall.

Need help?

If information is difficult to access, contact Tyler through the linked Facebook profile and describe the page and information needed.

Ongoing testing

Accessibility must be retested after real inventory photographs, contact details, forms, maps or third-party tools are added.
